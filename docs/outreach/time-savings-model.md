# Time-savings model (v0, estimates until pilots measure)

Every number here is an estimate from the two customer transcripts and job-ad descriptions. Label as "typical" in outreach, never as a measured result, until three pilots have logged actual minutes. Replace with measured values as they arrive.

## The coordinator's evening and morning, by hand

| Task, per operating day | Manual today | With DayRunner | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pull tomorrow's bookings from the booking system into a sheet | 15 min | 0 (synced) | Excel + manifest export, per operator call |
| Allocate vehicles and drivers, check licences, hours, availability | 30 to 45 min | 5 min review | Op Centre used to do this; coordinator does it by memory now |
| Pickup sequencing and times | 15 min | 0 | Route order from operator stop order |
| Guest pickup confirmations (1 to 2 min each, 25 pax) | 30 to 50 min | 5 min approve | WhatsApp/SMS one by one today |
| Supplier reconfirmations, 4 to 6 suppliers by email | 20 to 30 min | 3 min approve | Jackie: Gmail folders by category and day |
| Chasing supplier replies next morning | 10 to 20 min | 0 (replies matched) | Reads the thread in Gmail |
| Driver briefs, WhatsApp group post per driver | 10 to 15 min | 2 min | Operator call: WhatsApp group briefing |
| Driver hours and mileage log | 10 min | 0 (ledger) | Op Centre gap |
| **Total per day** | **2h 20m to 3h 20m** | **~15 min** | |

At 6 operating days a week: **14 to 20 hours a week** of coordinator time, reduced to about 1.5 hours. Multi-day charter tours are lower frequency but heavier per tour (a 5-day tour = 20 to 30 supplier confirmations plus rooming and day sheets).

## What that costs
- NZ operations coordinator: NZ$70k to 85k a year (Seek and Glassdoor, 2026). Nomad Safaris advertised NZ$65k to 80k for Operations and Fleet Coordinator in September 2026.
- 15 hours a week is 0.4 FTE, roughly NZ$28k to 34k a year of coordinator time, or the owner's evenings.
- Exception cost: one missed pickup or a guide double-booked can cost a full refund plus a review. Automate.travel claims €10,500 a year of "errors prevented" at 20k bookings; we make no such claim until measured.

## The three metrics to put in front of a prospect
1. **"Tomorrow's day, decided by 5pm."** Time from opening the app to an approved day: target under 15 minutes.
2. **"Hours you get back."** 14 to 20 hours a week for a 3 to 8 vehicle operator (estimate; will be measured in pilots).
3. **"Zero unconfirmed suppliers."** Every supplier for tomorrow shows confirmed, replied, or chased, before the day starts.

## How pilots measure
- Week 0: coordinator logs their evening/morning routine for 5 days (minutes per task). Two-line form in the pilot pack.
- Weeks 1 to 4: DayRunner logs plan time (open to approve), messages sent, confirmations returned, exceptions raised and resolved.
- Week 4 report: measured hours saved, exceptions caught, supplier reply rate. This becomes the case study and the price justification.
