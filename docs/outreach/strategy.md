# Outreach strategy: 60-day paid-pilot test

Goal by 5 November 2026: 3 paid pilots at NZ$500 to 800 a month, at least one charter or multi-day, plus 1 unsolicited referral. Lead list: docs/leads/nz-leads-2026-09-06.csv (233 operators).

## Positioning
Sell the coordinator, not the software. "Your ops coordinator, run by an agent, approved by you." Anchor to the job ad, not to Rezdy's US$49. Never say "operations layer" or "AI platform" to an operator.

One line: **Tomorrow's day, decided by 5pm.**

## Segments and hooks
| Segment | Hook | Proof in the demo |
|---|---|---|
| Own-fleet day tour and shuttle (3 to 15 vehicles) | "Who's driving tomorrow, and are they legal?" | Allocation with work-time check, exceptions, guest messages |
| Multi-day charter (Jackie's shape) | "Every supplier for day 3 confirmed, in one screen, from your own Gmail" | Supplier grouping by category and day, coach request, day sheet |
| Cruise-day surge operators (Tauranga, Dunedin, Akaroa) | "Cruise day: 400 pax, 12 vehicles, one board" | Split-vehicle allocation, driver briefs |
| Tongariro and ski shuttles | "Weather call at 5am: re-message everyone in two clicks" | Alerts field, re-drafted messages, hold |

## Tiers of the list
- **Tier 1 (priority 5 to 6, ~12 operators):** live hiring ad for drivers or an ops coordinator, open API platform, published contact. Personal video per operator. Call, not just email.
- **Tier 2 (priority 3 to 4, ~80):** FareHarbor or Rezdy detected, or a fleet size stated. Generic 45-second video plus personalised first line.
- **Tier 3 (rest):** in-house or unknown booking system. CSV-import pitch, batch email, low effort.

## Sequence (per operator, 21 days)
1. **Day 0, email 1.** Observation about them (their job ad, their departure count, a review mentioning pickups). One line on what DayRunner does. 45-second video link. Ask: 20-minute call. Under 120 words.
2. **Day 2, phone.** Call the published number between 10am and 12pm, after the morning departures. Ask for whoever does the run sheet. If voicemail, say the email subject line.
3. **Day 5, email 2.** Reply to email 1. One concrete metric from the model ("about 15 hours a week for a fleet your size, estimate"). Attach the 2.5-minute video. Same ask.
4. **Day 9, WhatsApp or SMS** if a mobile is published as the business line. Two sentences.
5. **Day 14, email 3.** The pilot offer in plain terms: NZ$X a month, I set up your bookings, four weeks, measured hours saved, cancel any time. Ask for a yes or no.
6. **Day 21, break-up.** "I'll stop here. If the 5pm job ever gets old, this link works." Close the loop.

Batch size: 15 new operators a week so calls stay personal. Tier 1 in week 1, Tier 2 weeks 2 to 5, Tier 3 weeks 6 to 8.

## The call (20 minutes)
1. 5 min: their evening routine. Who does it, how long, what tools. Write the minutes down; this is the baseline.
2. 10 min: demo on their shape. Show one exception, one approve-all, one supplier reply.
3. 5 min: the pilot offer and the price. Ask "what would stop you saying yes today."

## The pilot offer
- Four weeks, NZ$500 (Solo, up to 5 departures a day) or NZ$800 (Team) a month, paid monthly, cancel any time.
- Duncan sets up products, fleet, staff, suppliers, and connects the booking system or a weekly CSV. One-hour onboarding call. WhatsApp line to Duncan for the four weeks.
- Week 4 report: measured hours saved, exceptions caught, supplier reply rate.
- In return: permission to use the numbers and a quote, and one referral if it worked.

## Objections
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Rezdy already does allocation." | "It fills the first vehicle until it's full. It doesn't know Sam's on a 13-hour day or that the trailer's with the other van. Show me tomorrow in Rezdy and I'll show you the same day here." |
| "We just use WhatsApp and a spreadsheet." | "So does everyone I've talked to. That's the job I'm replacing. How long did it take last night?" |
| "Too expensive for software." | "It's not priced as software. A coordinator is NZ$70k. This is a fraction of that for the part of the job that happens after 5pm." |
| "We're on FareHarbor and you're not integrated." | "True, their API is partner-gated. Pilots run on a CSV export, five minutes a day, and I apply for the partnership with your name on it." |
| "AI sending messages to my guests?" | "It never sends. You approve. Every message is in your voice, and you can hold any one of them." |
| "Season's about to start, no time." | "That's exactly when the 5pm job costs the most. Setup is my time, not yours: one hour." |

## Channels beyond email
- Bókun App Store listing (only real marketplace in the category).
- Rezdy NZ operator roadshows (June 2026 recap on their blog; ask about the next one).
- TECNZ and TIA member newsletters: offer a "tomorrow's day in 15 minutes" webinar.
- Facebook groups: NZ Tour Operators, Tourism Industry Aotearoa members. Post the 45-second video once, no pitch, ask what their 5pm looks like.
- Referral ask at week 4 of every pilot.

## Tracking
Add columns to the lead CSV: status (new, emailed, called, demo booked, pilot, lost), last_touch, next_touch, owner_notes. Weekly numbers: emails sent, calls made, demos booked, pilots signed. Demo-to-pilot under 30% after ten demos means the price or the pitch is wrong; stop and fix before continuing.
