# Walkthrough video: script and shot list

Target: 2 min 30 s, screen recording with Duncan on camera in a small circle, no music, no intro card. Recorded once, cut into a 45-second version for cold email and a 2.5-minute version for the follow-up. Use the Kea Coach Tours demo (charter, multi-day) and the Remarkables Day Tours demo (own fleet) depending on the prospect. Default to Remarkables for shuttle and day-tour prospects.

## Opening (0:00 to 0:15), camera on
"Hi, I'm Duncan. If you run tours with more than a couple of vans, you know the 5pm job: who's driving tomorrow, who's been told their pickup time, and has the lunch stop actually confirmed. This is DayRunner. It does that job and hands you the decisions."

## Shot 1: The board (0:15 to 0:40), Day page for tomorrow
- Show the summary strip: departures, pax, km, work hours.
- "It's already pulled tomorrow's bookings from Rezdy. It's allocated the vans and drivers, and it's checked the work-time rules, so nobody's on a 14-hour day."
- Hover the allocation row showing km and work minutes.

## Shot 2: The exceptions (0:40 to 1:05)
- Scroll to the exceptions list.
- "Here's the bit that matters. It doesn't bury you in a dashboard. It gives you the three things that need a decision: this departure is one seat over, this driver hits the 5.5-hour break, this supplier hasn't replied. Each one has options. You pick, it moves on."
- Click one option, show it resolve.

## Shot 3: Messages (1:05 to 1:30)
- Scroll to the message queue.
- "Every guest gets a pickup message drafted in your voice. Nothing sends until you approve. Approve all, or hold one. Reply 1 to confirm comes back into the board."
- Click Approve all. Show status change.

## Shot 4: Suppliers (1:30 to 1:55)
- Suppliers grouped by category and day.
- "Reconfirmations go out from your own Gmail, grouped the way you already file them: meals, activities, accommodation, transport, by day. When the hotel replies, DayRunner reads the thread and marks it confirmed. You stop chasing."
- Show Bluewater replied, one still awaiting.

## Shot 5: Driver sheet (1:55 to 2:15), Run Sheet page
- "Each driver gets their own brief. Pickups in order, notes only they need to see, and the day sheet for the guide on a multi-day tour, with the group, rooming, and what's next tomorrow. Print it or send it."

## Close (2:15 to 2:30), camera on
"That's the whole thing: tomorrow's day, decided by 5pm, about 15 minutes instead of two or three hours. I'm running three paid pilots in New Zealand this spring. If you want to be one, reply and I'll set your bookings up myself."

## Production notes
- Record at 1440 x 900, browser zoomed 110%, sidebar visible. Demo logins in docs/RUNSHEET.md.
- Reseed both demos before recording (app/seed-demo.mjs, app/seed-demo-tours.mjs) so dates read as tomorrow.
- Cursor slow. Every click is a sentence.
- 45-second cut: opening, shot 2, shot 3, close.
- Export MP4 plus a 6-second silent GIF of the exceptions list resolving for the email body.
- Host on the landing page under /demo and as an unlisted YouTube link for email.
